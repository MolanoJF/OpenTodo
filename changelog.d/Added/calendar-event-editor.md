- **Add events to your calendar, edit all their details, duplicate them or turn them into tasks.** In Day and Week, double-click, drag over an empty stretch, press `q` or `Shift+Q`, or choose **New event** in the command bar to add an event to your Google, Outlook or CalDAV calendar; a side panel edits its title, times, time zone, calendar, location, description and busy or free, with **Duplicate** and **Convert to task**. It stays off until you turn on **Edit events in OpenTodo** on the connection in **Settings → Integrations → Calendar sync**. Repeating events, reminders and guests are not part of it. Assistants and `ot calendar event add` can create events too.
