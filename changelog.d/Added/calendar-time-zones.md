- **See a second time zone beside the hours of Day and Week.** In **Settings → Language and region → Calendar grid**, choose a **Second time zone** and the grid shows what time it is there next to its own hour labels, with a short code (BOG, BER) above each column. It is off until you pick a zone, belongs to each device, and reads half-hour and 45-minute zones such as India and Nepal correctly, including across daylight-saving changes.
