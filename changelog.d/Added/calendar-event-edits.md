- **Move, resize, rename and delete your calendar events in the calendar.** Drag an event of a connected Google, Outlook or CalDAV calendar in Day or Week, use the arrow keys, or click it to change its title and times; Delete removes it, with Undo for a few seconds. It is off until you turn on **Edit events in OpenTodo** on the connection in **Settings → Integrations → Calendar sync**. Repeating events, events with guests and read-only calendars stay read-only and say why; if the event changed in your calendar meanwhile, your edit is not applied. Assistants and `ot calendar event edit` can do it too.
