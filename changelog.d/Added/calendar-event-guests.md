- **See who is invited, answer invitations and join calls from your calendar.** Events of a connected Google, Outlook or CalDAV calendar show their organiser and guests, and a **Join** button opens the call (Google Meet, Teams, Zoom or Webex) from the event, the editor and Today. On Outlook and Microsoft 365 you can answer an invitation with **Yes**, **Maybe** or **No**, change a meeting you organise or invite guests after one "Tell 3 guests?" confirmation (Outlook sends the message itself, so there is no way to send nothing), and add a Teams meeting. Google replies are built but off until checked on a real calendar, and events with guests on Google and other CalDAV calendars stay read-only. Guest names and addresses are cached on your server and never logged or shown to the in-app assistant. Assistants and `ot calendar event` take guests and a new `respond` command.
