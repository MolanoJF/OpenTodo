- **Change repeating calendar events, set how they repeat and add reminders.** Move, resize, rename or delete one occurrence of a repeating event in your Google, Outlook or CalDAV calendar and choose **This event**, **This and following events** or **All events**; the choice is asked after a drag, the arrow keys, a rename, **Save** and **Delete**. In the event editor and when you add an event, set **Repeat** (every day, weekday, week, month or year, any interval, ending never, on a date or after a number of times) and **Reminders**, which your calendar app sends. There is no Undo for **This and following events**, and a repeat OpenTodo can't write back without loss stays read-only. Assistants and `ot calendar event` take `scope`, `repeat` and reminders too.
