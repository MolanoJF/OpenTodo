# Why
The calendar toolbar, quarter-hour rules, capacity totals and empty dashed lanes add visual noise. Match the supplied Notion Calendar references while retaining task scheduling and mobile usability.

# What
Replace the segmented range switch with a checked dropdown and a Number of days step (2–6 days). Separate Today from previous/next arrows. Put completed-task and external-calendar visibility in More actions. Remove weekend shading and quarter-hour rules, center day headings, hide capacity totals in Day/Week, and leave empty all-day lanes unlabelled. Week headings show month/year and ISO week number.
